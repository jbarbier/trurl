TRURL ON A USB STICK
====================

This stick installs Trurl and its models. No internet is needed.

ON A WINDOWS 10 OR 11 COMPUTER

1. Double-click Trurl_..._x64-offline-setup.exe
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
   "Run anyway". (Trurl is safe; this message shows for new programs.)
3. Click Next until it finishes. It copies the models from the "models" folder:
   this takes 2 to 6 minutes. Do not remove the stick until it says it is done.
4. Open Trurl from the Start menu.

No administrator password is needed. Trurl installs for the current user only.

ON A LINUX COMPUTER (if the "linux" folder is on this stick)

1. Open a terminal in the "linux" folder.
2. tar xzf trurl-*-linux-x64.tar.gz
3. sh trurl-*-linux-x64/install.sh
   (it also copies the models from this stick)
4. Type: trurl

WHAT IS ON THIS STICK

  models/             Gemma 3 1B (806 MB), Gemma 3 4B (2.5 GB), Qwen3 0.6B (484 MB)
  LICENSES/           Gemma Terms of Use and every other license
  SHA256SUMS.txt      to check that a copy is complete

If a computer has only 4 GB of memory, use Gemma 3 1B or Qwen3 0.6B. The model
list in Trurl shows a green dot for models that fit easily.

To check a copied stick on Linux:  sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt
